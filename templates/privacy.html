1. Data collected

To provide the service we store: username, email (optional), a PBKDF2-hashed password, your preferences, and the flight telemetry (CSV logs and derived tracks/statistics) you upload. IP addresses may be recorded in the audit log for security.

2. Purpose

Your data is used exclusively to run and improve this flight-log analysis service. It is stored on this server and is not sold or shared with third parties.

3. Data retention & deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Account page; this also removes your flights, vehicles and uploaded files. A final backup may be created before deletion at your request.

4. Your rights (GDPR)

  • Access (art. 15) — request a copy of your data at any time from the Account page.
  • Rectification (art. 16) — edit your username, email and preferences.
  • Erasure (art. 17) — delete your account and data.
  • Data portability (art. 20) — export your data in JSON.

5. Contact

For any privacy request, contact the site administrator.
